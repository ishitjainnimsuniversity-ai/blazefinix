# BlazeFinix — UI/UX Pro Max Redesign Report

## 1. Design System Transformation

Following `ui-ux-pro-max` intelligence recommendations for **Healthcare Clinical Applications** (`pattern: Social Proof + Clinical Hierarchy`, `style: Neumorphism/Light Medical + Data-Dense`, `typography: Figtree / Inter`, `colors: Medical Cyan #0891B2, Slate 50/900 neutrals, Emerald #059669, Amber #D97706, Red #DC2626`), BlazeFinix has been completely rebuilt with a dual visual architecture.

---

## 2. Navigation Architecture

Consolidated 17 scattered navigation items into a logical 2-tier clinical architecture:

### 🏥 Clinical Workspace (Light Medical Theme)
- **Overview:** Clinical operations overview, pending review queue table, active cohort statistics, decision support engine status.
- **Patients & Cohorts:** Dense cohort dataset table with search and quick report downloads.
- **New Assessment:** Guided workflow with PDF report extraction or grouped parameter entry.
- **Decision Support:** Flagship clinical result page (Patient Context -> Risk Stratification -> SHAP Biomarker Drivers -> Interpretation -> Collapsible Technical Drawer).
- **Alerts & Review:** Clinical triage queue with severity badges and physician review logging.
- **Clinical Reports:** Clean printable dossier and PDF generator.

### 🔬 Research & Platform (Dark Technical Theme)
- **Model Benchmarks:** Empirical model comparisons, ROC-AUC curves, latency metrics.
- **Quantum Circuit Lab:** 4-Qubit parameterized ansatz diagram, Bloch vector state telemetry.
- **Genomics & Data:** Live NCBI/Ensembl driver gene lookup and dataset provenance.
- **Audit & Settings:** Compliance audit log trail and risk threshold controls.

---

## 3. Terminology & Medical Integrity Corrections

1. **"Real Patients" -> "Research Cohort Records / TCGA Dataset Records":** Prevented research dataset records from being misidentified as active clinical patients.
2. **"Publication-Grade Reports" -> "Clinical Decision Reports":** Removed overclaiming marketing language.
3. **No Fabricated Confidence:** Real epistemic uncertainty scores (`0.0861`) and model scores are honestly presented without fake 100% medical certainty.
